Position: Bookkeeper

Full time | 30 hours per week | $35-$37 per hour


About the Role

We are seeking an experienced and detail-oriented Bookkeeper to join our team. The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining accurate financial records, managing day to day bookkeeping activities, and supporting financial reporting across our New Zealand and Australian business operations.

This role requires strong accounting knowledge, excellent organisational skills, and the ability to independently manage financial records, reconcile transactions and resolve discrepancies.

Key Responsibilities

  • Maintaining accurate and up to date financial records, including recording and classifying financial transactions using accounting software.

  • Performing bank reconciliations, general ledger reconciliations and month end bookkeeping procedures.

  • Managing accounts payable and receivable, including processing invoices, recording payments and monitoring outstanding balances.

  • Maintaining and reconciling intercompany accounts and transactions between New Zealand and Australian business entities.

  • Reviewing and reconciling inventory related financial transactions, stock purchases and associated costs against accounting records.

  • Preparing regular financial summaries, reconciliation reports and supporting documentation for management and external accountants.

  • Assisting with GST reporting, financial record preparation and other routine accounting compliance requirements.

  • Investigating financial discrepancies, correcting accounting records and ensuring the accuracy and completeness of financial information.

  • Liaising with suppliers and internal teams regarding invoices, payment records and financial discrepancies.

  • Supporting improvements to bookkeeping procedures, accounting systems and financial record management.

About You

  • A Diploma in Accounting or a related field, or at least two years of practical bookkeeping experience.

  • Sound knowledge of bookkeeping principles, financial reconciliations and accounting procedures.

  • Experience using accounting software and maintaining financial records.

  • Strong numerical accuracy, analytical skills and attention to detail.

  • Ability to work independently, manage competing deadlines and resolve financial discrepancies.

  • Excellent communication and organisational skills.

About the Role

We're looking for an Accounts Payable Officer to join our Finance team at Overland Footwear Group. Supporting our New Zealand and Australian operations, you'll play an important role in managing accounts payable processes, ensuring invoices and payments are processed accurately and on time. This is a great opportunity for someone who enjoys working in a fast-paced environment, takes pride in accuracy and enjoys building strong working relationships across the business.


What you'll be doing

  • Manage end-to-end accounts payable processing, ensuring invoices are accurately coded, approved, and processed within agreed timeframes.
  • Prepare and complete scheduled payment runs for New Zealand and Australian entities, including overseas payments.
  • Review supplier statements, investigate discrepancies and follow up on invoice irregularities.
  • Maintain accurate supplier records and payment details within the system.
  • Reconcile and process company credit card transactions, including BNZ Visa, NAB Visa and AMEX.
  • Ensure all payments are supported by the appropriate documentation and authorisations, with remittances sent to suppliers following payment runs.
  • Review and process employee expense claims, ensuring supporting documentation and approvals are in place.
  • Prepare and distribute monthly sales reports to relevant retail malls, ensuring reporting is accurate and submitted on time.
  • Provide support across the wider Finance team and assist with ad hoc tasks as required.


What we're looking for

  • Previous experience in an Accounts Payable or similar finance administration role.
  • Strong attention to detail and a high level of accuracy when processing financial information.
  • Good organisational and time management skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities and meet deadlines.
  • Strong communication skills and confidence liaising with internal stakeholders and external suppliers.
  • A proactive approach to problem-solving, with the ability to identify and follow up on discrepancies.
  • Comfortable working independently while contributing positively to a collaborative team environment.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and experience using accounting or ERP systems.
