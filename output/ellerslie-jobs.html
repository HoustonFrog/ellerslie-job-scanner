Assistant Accountant

Full-time, HRV NZ – Auckland (Onehunga)

Job Description

At HRV, we believe everyone should love the feeling of coming home. Since 2003, we’ve helped over 500,000 Kiwi households enjoy warmer, drier and healthier homes. Our purpose is simple: Making all Kiwi Homes Healthier.

We’re looking for an experienced Assistant Accountant to join our Auckland-based Finance team. This is a key role supporting the delivery of accurate, timely financial information and ensuring strong financial processes across the business.

About the role

Reporting to the Financial Manager, you’ll play a hands-on role across accounts receivable, assistant accounting, and month-end processes, while also supporting continuous improvement of systems and reporting.

You’ll work closely with internal stakeholders across the business and help ensure the finance function delivers reliable, high-quality support every day.

Your key responsibilities will include:

Accounts Receivable

  • Managing daily, weekly and monthly payment allocations across multiple channels (EFTPOS, Paystation, QCard, Eroad and others)

  • Maintaining accurate bank reconciliations and suspense accounts

  • Administering customer refunds, transfers and payment reallocations

  • Ensuring accuracy of invoicing, remittances and franchisee recharges

Assistant Accounting

  • Supporting month-end processes including journals, reconciliations, amortisation, depreciation, FX updates and fixed asset reporting

  • Preparing financial and cost centre reports, including P&L reporting for internal stakeholders

  • Preparing and reviewing GST returns and other statutory reports

  • Supporting ad hoc financial analysis and ensuring data integrity

  • Championing financial systems such as Business Central, Visa Intellilink, Insight and Eroad

  • Identifying and implementing process and reporting efficiencies, including automation initiatives

  • Providing backup support across accounts payable, payroll and credit control as required

General Finance & Business Support

  • Delivering a professional and positive experience to internal and external stakeholders

  • Supporting a collaborative, high-performing team environment

  • Acting as a trusted ambassador for HRV and modelling our values

  • Managing priorities effectively to meet deadlines and business needs

Health, safety and wellbeing

At HRV, the health, safety and wellbeing of our people is a top priority. You’ll be expected to work safely, follow all health and safety requirements, and actively support a culture where everyone loves the feeling of getting home safe, every day.

Who are we looking for?

We’re looking for a capable, detail-oriented finance professional who enjoys working in a collaborative environment and takes pride in delivering high-quality work.

You’ll be a great fit if you have:

  • A minimum of 5 years’ experience in accounts receivable and assistant accounting roles

  • Strong experience with month-end processes and reconciliations

  • Experience preparing GST returns and statutory reporting

  • Confidence working with financial systems (experience with Business Central is highly regarded)

  • Excellent attention to detail and strong organisational skills

  • Clear, professional communication skills

  • A proactive mindset with a focus on continuous improvement

  • The ability to manage competing priorities and meet deadlines

What’s in it for you?

  • A full-time role with a well-established, trusted New Zealand brand

  • A supportive, values-led team aligned to the HRV Way

  • A role with real variety and responsibility across the finance function

  • Opportunities to improve systems and processes

  • Ongoing learning and development opportunities

Additional information

  • This is a permanent, full-time employee role

  • Location: Auckland (Onehunga)

  • Applicants must have the legal right to work in New Zealand

  • Pre-employment checks may apply


HRV is committed to building a diverse, inclusive and respectful workplace where our people feel supported, valued and proud of the work they do.
